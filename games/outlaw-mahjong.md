# Outlaw Mahjong (水浒麻将)

**Genre:** Mahjong roguelike deckbuilder · **Platform:** Browser · **Price:** Free · **Languages:** English, 中文

Outlaw Mahjong turns mahjong into a short roguelike run. You never need a full fourteen-tile winning hand. Instead you choose one to six tiles from a thirteen-tile hand and play the best pattern you can see, such as a pair, a chow, a pung, two melds or a kong. The pattern sets your base chips and multiplier, and each scoring tile adds its face value.

Between blinds you spend coins to recruit heroes from the 108 outlaws of the Water Margin. They sit in five flower-tile slots and trigger from left to right, so seating order matters. Every third blind is a boss from the Song court, such as Gao Qiu, Tong Guan or Cai Jing, each with a rule that breaks a favourite strategy. A full run of eight antes takes about thirty minutes, and there is a daily leaderboard.

**Play:** [Outlaw Mahjong – mahjong card game in your browser](https://mahjongcardgame.com)
