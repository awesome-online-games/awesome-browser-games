# Shmup Rogue

**Genre:** Vertical shoot 'em up / roguelike · **Platform:** Browser (keyboard, touch or gamepad) · **Price:** Free

Shmup Rogue blends a 90s-style vertical scrolling arcade shooter with wave-based roguelike build crafting. Your fighter crosses oceans, coastlines, cities and deep space while helicopters, bombers, tanks and turrets fill the screen with bullets. You dodge, grab power-ups, cycle main guns with coloured crystals and save bombs for emergencies.

A run lasts 20 timed waves. Between waves you spend the scrap you collected in the hangar shop on weapon pods, items and upgrades, so every run turns into a different build. Several ships, boss fights and adjustable danger levels keep runs varied. It runs on desktop and mobile in five languages, with no download or account required.

**Play:** [Shmup Rogue – free shoot 'em up roguelike](https://shmup.org)
