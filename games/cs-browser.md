# CS Browser

**Genre:** Team shooter · **Platform:** Browser (desktop and mobile) · **Price:** Free

CS Browser is an independent team shooter made for people who grew up on Counter-Strike 1.6. A match starts in seconds inside a browser tab, with no install and no account. Quick match drops you onto Cargo Ship, a compact container deck, while the lobby also offers Dust 2 for classic bomb defusal rounds. If you prefer constant action, team deathmatch gives fast respawns instead.

Bomb defusal includes a full buy menu laid out the way veterans expect: pistols, shotguns, SMGs, rifles, a machine gun, grenades, armor and a defuse kit. Controls use WASD and mouse with rebindable keys, and phones get touch and gyro aiming. You can share a room link so friends join a 4v4 game in one click, and bots fill any empty seats.

**Play:** [CS Browser – play CS online in your browser](https://playcsbrowser.com)
